# Third-party notices

## Inspiration

Trazo is an independent implementation. It borrows ideas, not code, from two MIT-licensed projects:

- **archify** by tt-a1i (https://github.com/tt-a1i/archify): diagrams as a typed JSON spec, validation
  that fails before delivery with an actionable diagnosis, sources bound to repository files and lines,
  a viewer with theme, zoom, search and focus, and build receipts.
- **diagram-design** by Cathryn Lavery (https://github.com/cathrynlavery/diagram-design): the minimal
  editorial style, the connector rules (orthogonal rounded elbows, masked labels with a visible gap, no
  overlapping connectors, spread attach points, no lines behind unrelated nodes), the complexity budget,
  the editorial accent, the 4px grid and the accessible SVG contract. The default color palette follows
  its default skin.
